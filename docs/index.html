The KL budget as a robustness radius

An adversary picks a distribution ν of single-site fields, as far from the Gaussian μ as a KL budget H(ν | μ) ≤ ρ allows, to maximise Ω(ν) = Φ(Eνφ²) + Eνf − H(ν | μ). For the REM approximation of the SK TAP free energy this worst case equals the Parisi formula. Move the sliders and watch the two sides agree, and the budget switch from slack to binding.

Worst-case distribution vs nominal

The optimal ν* is an exponential tilt of μ: ν*(dx) ∝ exp(m*[Φ′(q*)φ²(x) + f(x)]) μ(dx), a softmax reweighting at temperature 1/m*.

What you are seeing

Why it matters: in DRO the radius ρ says how far the true distribution may be from the nominal one. Here it has a physical meaning. By Sanov's theorem, about 2Ne−N·H(ν|μ) of the 2N configurations have empirical measure near ν, so measures with H > log 2 are simply not realised. The budget is the entropy of the configuration space.

The Parisi side: minimise over m

P along the KKT path, with q = q*(m) solving q = Eν[φ²] for each m. The minimum is the worst-case value. λ = 1/m is the KL multiplier of the DRO dual.

Complementary slackness

KL used by the Gibbs measure νq*(m),m. Since ∂P/∂m = (H − ρ)/m², the optimum sits where H crosses ρ, or at m = 1 if it never does.
